In a snap decision, you start pulling yourself to the doors to your left. 
One foot and hand at a time until you reach the doors. 

You slam your palm against the emergency panel.
The outer bulkhead seals grind shut, locking you out of the cargo bay.
Through the small viewport, you see the containment field flickering—
blue light spasming like a failing heartbeat.

You brace yourself against the wall and hit Eject Cargo.

For a second, nothing happens.
Then a deep rumble tears through the hull as crates, containers, 
and an entire section of the bay rip free, vanishing into the black beyond.

For a moment you stare through the small view port. 
The cargo bay’s gone.
Then you start, But the ship—what’s left of it—still lives. 
Time to see if you can keep in that way

You stagger toward Engineering, following the maintenance signs etched into the bulkheads.
Each step feels heavier, the air hotter, the deck plates vibrating like the ship’s barely holding together.
You pass shattered displays, flickering lights, the dull metallic groan of a vessel under unbearable strain.

By the time you reach the core chamber, your throat tastes like metal.
The air smells of ozone and burnt insulation.

You stumble inside. The reactor looms before you—a giant sphere of cracked alloy, pulsing faintly.
Power levels: 7% and dropping.

You move quickly.
Pulling up access panels, rerouting circuits, anything to buy the ship a few more minutes of life.
A warning flashes across the display: 
------------------------------
⚠ SHIELD INTEGRITY COMPROMISED
⚠ GRAVITATIONAL STRESS CRITICAL.
------------------------------
You’re still trying to parse it when you hear it—footsteps. Behind you. 
“…You… How?” a voice shakily says

You spin to see someone standing in the doorway.
Breathing hard.

It’s you.

For a moment, the world tilts again—
two sets of eyes, both wide, both disbelieving.

He cuts himself off.
“What does that matter, we will figure it out later. 
For now, how are we going to restore power.”

You both move at once, no questions, no hesitation.
Tools clatter. Sparks fly.
You shout readings, they patch conduits, and for the first time, the systems respond.

Power surges back through the lines.
The ship’s hum deepens, growing steadier.
On the screen, the numbers climb—12%… 28%… 46%…

Then, for a heartbeat, everything feels possible.

But the ship groans again—a deep, sickening sound from the hull.
The deck trembles.
A warning screams across every console: 
--------------------------
⚠ HULL INTEGRITY COMPRAMIZED
⚠ SHIELD RESTORATION ADVISED
--------------------------
You both grab for stability as the lights flash, the core pulsing wildly.
Your double meets your eyes through the chaos.
Neither of you says it out loud, but you both know:

You’re running out of time.

Together, you reach for the controls

— End of Part 1 —
