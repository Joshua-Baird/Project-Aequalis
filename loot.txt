You swallow hard and step closer to the body. The smell is still thick in the air—metallic, burnt, and wrong.
You crouch beside the corpse, trying not to look at the face. The blackened skin makes it difficult to tell much anyway.

You reach for their belt. There’s a faint glint beneath the charred fabric—a data pad, scorched along one edge but still faintly glowing. You ease it free.

It flickers to life, projecting fractured text across the air.
For a moment, all you see are errors and half-broken system logs. Then a warning flashes:
-----------------------------------------------------
⚠ WARNING: GRAVITATIONAL DISTORTION DETECTED
PROXIMITY ALERT — SINGULARITY WITHIN RANGE
VESSEL POSITION: PERIAPSIS STABLE
-----------------------------------------------------
You stare at the words. Singularity?
That means black hole.

You glance up toward the ceiling — as if you could see it through the ship’s hull. The faint hum beneath the floor makes more sense now.
The groaning metal, the pull in your stomach… the entire ship is on the edge of a black hole’s event horizon.

Your breath catches. The body beside you crackles faintly as you shift it to look for anything else.
The skin flakes where your glove brushes it, revealing muscle, then—

You freeze.

Beneath the burns, the jawline, the shape of the cheekbones—
They’re yours.
You stumble back, almost dropping the data pad.
It can’t be. You just woke up. You just got here.

But the more you look, the more impossible it becomes to deny. The faint scar above the left brow.
The same haircut, the same mole beneath the right ear—burned, but still visible.

This is you.
Or something that looks exactly like you.
You glance down at the data pad again, hands shaking.

Maybe it has answers.
You swipe through the broken interface, flipping through half-corrupted logs until one boots up:
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROJECT AEQUALIS // TRANSPORTATION RESEARCH DIVISION
Status: Experimental
Directive: Temporal-spatial bifurcation trials via singularity-assisted transport.
Subject Stability: Degrading
Notes: Matter transmission near gravitational convergence has resulted in replication. 
Cellular integrity weakens proportionally to exposure.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The screen glitches, stuttering with static.

Your eyes scan the words again. Replication events. Cellular degradation.
Your hand trembles as the meaning hits.
You didn’t just kill someone.
You killed yourself.

Or… one version of you.
The thought sits heavy, hollow in your chest.

The ship groans again, a deep metallic rumble that rattles the floor. A nearby conduit sparks, plunging part of the corridor into darkness.
The datapad chimes weakly:
-------------------------------
⚠ ENERGY LEVELS CRITICAL
⚠ SHIELD INTEGRITY COMPROMISED
-------------------------------

The air hums around you. You have to move—now.
You can’t think here. Not with that thing’s eyes staring back at you.

You glance at the datapad map overlay, two routes blinking faintly in the haze of static:
1. Escape Pods — You could leave. Get off this cursed ship.
2. Engineering — Maybe restore power, figure out what happened here. Maybe fix it.

What do you do?
